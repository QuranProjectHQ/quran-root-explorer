# VISION — a global project for humanity

This project is offered freely to all people. It belongs to no company and to no single person; its
work is dedicated to the public domain so that anyone, anywhere, may read it, use it, learn from it,
and build on it — without cost, login, or permission.

## What we are reaching for
- **Free and open, for everyone.** No paywalls, no accounts, no borders. The text, the tools, the
  findings, and the teaching are a shared inheritance.
- **Honest before impressive.** This is an evidence instrument, not a claims engine. Every result is
  shown with its statistic, its baseline, and its honest verdict — including the many that come back
  null. We do not overclaim, and we do not use a "miracle" voice. Trust is earned by being truthful.
- **Built to grow.** The architecture invites the world to contribute — new lenses, new languages,
  new translations, corrections — while a careful review process keeps it sound. It is designed to
  outlast any one contributor (see `SUCCESSION.md`).
- **Built to last.** Public, mirrored, and free to host, so it cannot quietly disappear.

## The guiding spirit — oneness
The founder's inspiration is the concept of **oneness**: that knowledge, like humanity, is ultimately
one — meant to be shared rather than divided, unifying rather than walling people off. In practice
that spirit shows up as concrete choices: one open home for everything; one freely-shared body of
work; one community, across languages and backgrounds, welcomed on equal terms; credit honestly given
to everyone whose work we build on. The research itself stays strictly data-driven and makes no
theological claims as findings — but the *reason* the project is given away freely, to all of
humanity, is this aspiration toward unity.

## Our commitments
- Keep it free and public — always.
- Keep it honest — evidence first, nulls shown, no overclaiming.
- Keep it open — welcome the world's contributions, guarded by review.
- Keep it credited — honor every source (`SOURCES.md`).
- Keep it alive — maintain succession so it never depends on one person (`SUCCESSION.md`).
