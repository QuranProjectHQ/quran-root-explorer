# The Disjoint Letters (al-Muqaṭṭaʿāt) — 17-Lecture Course Plan

A full-semester university course, parallel in standard to the **signal** and **biology** courses, on a single validated latent feature of the Qur'an: the disjoint letters as a **positional / organizational pointer** indexing contiguous sūra-families in both muṣḥaf and revelation order.

**Locked standards (inherited):** ≥20 editable slides per deck · ≥50% visual (≥10 real charts per deck, computed from Book6.xlsx) · root anchor (ریشه) · audit ✓/✗/~ · Monte-Carlo / label-permutation null where testable · *Real-world relevance & takeaway* each lecture · no "scientific-miracle" claims · per-lecture kit (exercise+key, quiz+key, app guide, instructor script). No filler — every lecture carries distinct content.

**Meta-thesis:** the Qur'an's detectable latent structure is **relational/organizational, not in local content statistics**. The disjoint letters are the project's clearest, all-inclusive confirmation (omnibus label-permutation p ≈ 2×10⁻⁵ over all 29 muqaṭṭaʿāt sūras).

## Unit A — Foundations
1. **Introduction** — the mystery letters; the pointer hypothesis; what would falsify it.
2. **The Method** — the label-permutation null; the false positive; the five-gate gauntlet.
3. **The Data & the Root Anchor** — Book6.xlsx; building the 29-sūra family table; verify before validate.

## Unit B — The core result
4. **Contiguity I — Book Order (muṣḥaf)** — within-family distance; the omnibus p ≈ 2×10⁻⁵.
5. **Contiguity II — Revelation Order (nuzūl)** — the independent confirmation; nuzūl reconstruction & its caveats.
6. **Per-Family Deep Dive** — ḤM, ALM, ALR, ṬSM each tested in both orders; no cherry-picking.
7. **The Long-Sūra Flag** — muqaṭṭaʿāt mark the major sūras (median 85 vs 26, p ≈ 2×10⁻⁵); but length is not per-tag.
8. **Revelation-Phase Mapping** — simple tags early, families late, المر alone in Medina.

## Unit C — Statistical machinery
9. **Permutation Tests in Depth** — exchangeability, the null distribution, why freeze-and-shuffle isolates the tag.
10. **Multiple Comparisons & FDR** — the look-elsewhere effect; Benjamini–Hochberg; declaring tests in advance.
11. **Effect Size, Power & the Scale Rule** — beyond p-values; why singletons can't be tested; stability with n.
12. **Bootstrap & Confidence** — resampling the families; confidence intervals on the within-family distance.

## Unit D — What it is NOT
13. **No Shared Theme** — root-profile similarity within ≈ cross (p ≈ 0.27); distinctive roots are flavor, not finding.
14. **No Frequency Code** — the within-chapter false positive vs the cross-chapter baseline (0/29).
15. **The Single-Letter Leads (ق, ن)** — the honest partial: Qāf 3rd-densest of 114; borderline after correction.

## Unit E — Context & synthesis
16. **Boundary Variants & the Graph View** — المص, المر at the seams; the sūra-family network.
17. **Synthesis & the Pointer Model** — the validated positional index; meta-thesis across biology, signal, DL.
