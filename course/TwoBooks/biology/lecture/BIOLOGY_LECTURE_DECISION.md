**Modules (8 beats each; beat 6 = real Book6 datum):**

8. Synthesis — composition is real but dominated by common letters + length; grammar
   leaves an adjacency footprint; no hidden code.

**Honest spine:** the genome lens is a *measurement frame*, not a claim of biological
design. Base composition is governed by the commonest letters; richness is governed by
length; both must be read against size. What is genuinely informative is the *grammar
footprint* — di-codon bias and the drop in conditional entropy (H₀→H₁) — which is what
any natural language shows. Permutation/shuffle nulls throughout; no miracle claims.
