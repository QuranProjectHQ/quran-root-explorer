# The Two Books (Qur'an ↔ Biology) — Course Index

A 17-lecture, 3-hour-per-session university course reading the Book of Scripture (the Qur'an, *tadwīn*) beside the Book of Creation (the living cell, *takwīn*) as two works of **one Author**, compared **as structure** — never as identity. Every parallel is audited **✓ Supported / ✗ Breaks / ~ Silent**, and the one inferential claim (Pillar 2) is validated against a Monte-Carlo null (**p < 0.003**).

**Central thesis (three pillars):** a tiny alphabet of *meaningless* units, through **(1) generativity** (few → many), **(2) order** (sequence makes meaning), and **(3) expression** (selection realizes diversity), builds an unbounded, organized, networked, conserved, self-correcting archive addressed to a responsible reader — found, on real data, in **both** Books. (See `THESIS_EVIDENCE.md`.)

## Data provenance (read before citing a figure)

- **Qur'an figures** are computed from `../../Book6.xlsx`. **Important:** Book6's "root" column (col 9) is a normalized **stem** column, not strict triliteral lemmas (e.g., the stem جن conflates *jinn* with *janna*/garden), and it uses Persian letter forms (ی/ک). Counts are therefore **stem-token counts**. Verified exact stem counts used in the decks include: ṣabr صبر **28** tokens / 20 surahs; baṣar بصر 12; ghayb غيب **49**; shahid شهد **17**; nafs نفس **126**; rūḥ روح 23; qalb قلب 20; raḥma رحمة 114; raḥmān رحمن 57.
- **Biology figures** are mainstream/textbook, given in round form. Charts marked *"approximate," "illustrative,"* or *"schematic"* are pedagogical magnitudes, not measured values, and are labelled as such on the slide.
- The headline validations are real computations: the anagram-sibling rate **54%** vs a frequency-matched null **44.7%** (95th 47%), **p < 0.003** (0 of 300 draws); Pillar-1/3 amplification and sparsity are direct censuses.
