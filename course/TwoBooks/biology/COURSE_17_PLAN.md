# The Two Books (Qur'an ↔ Biology) — a 17-Lecture University Course

**عالم التدوين (قول الله, the Qur'an)  ·  عالم التكوين (فعل الله, the living cell)** — one Author, one addressee (the human; the jinn later), both *āyāt*.

> **Scope.** The **Qur'an ↔ molecular-biology** module — self-contained, climbing one ladder: symbol → code → sequence → meaning → process → system → addressee. The **ayah-as-signal (1-D)** and **surah-as-image (2-D)** treatments are **separate future Two Books courses** (`../TwoBooks_AyahSignal_1D/`, `../TwoBooks_SurahImage_2D/`), not part of this one.

A 3-hour-per-session course. Reads the Book of Scripture beside the Book of Creation, comparing **structure** with discipline — never claiming miracle, prediction, or encoding. Every parallel is audited **✓ Supported / ✗ Breaks / ~ Silent**, every figure traces to real data from **both** domains, and any pattern-claim must beat a **Monte-Carlo null**.

## The 17 lectures — a simple → complex journey

The order is a single climb: each lecture assumes only what came before. Numbers match folder names `01_…`–`17_…`.

### Unit A — Foundations & substrate (1–4)
1. **Introduction** — tadwīn/takwīn, one Author, the audit discipline, the roadmap.
2. **The Method** — Verification vs Validation, the Monte-Carlo null, the six traps and their guards.
3. **Alphabets & Codes** — the raw symbols first: 30 letters vs 4 bases / 64 codons / 20 amino acids; sparse triplet lexicons.
4. **The Mapping Problem** — *now that we know the alphabets,* can letters map to codons/amino acids? A V&V case study (cardinality mismatch, sampled null, why identity-maps fail).

### Unit B — The character/sequence scale, no semantics (5–6)
5. **Codon & Root** — the triplet code in both Books; position-matters statistics; degeneracy.
6. **Word & Peptide** — building sequences from a small alphabet; length distributions.

### Unit C — The semantic scale, meaning enters (7–9)
7. **Root & Protein** — structure folds into function/meaning.
8. **Concept Fields & Functional Domains** — semantic families ↔ protein families/folds. *(Midterm after this.)*
9. **Variation** — diacritics ↔ point mutations; a root's forms ↔ a gene's isoforms.

### Unit D — Process & expression (10–12)
10. **Istinsākh & Genomics** — copying, transcription, translation.
11. **Fidelity & Preservation** — accuracy of copying; ḥifẓ ↔ proofreading & repair; the real point of difference.
12. **Regulation & Context** — when a word/gene is "expressed"; revelation order ↔ developmental timing; context-dependent sense ↔ tissue-specific expression.

### Unit E — System-level architecture (13–15)
13. **Genome & Muṣḥaf** — whole-corpus architecture: chromosomes ↔ sūras; gene density / GC ↔ letter & root distribution.
14. **Networks & Interactions** — protein–protein interaction networks ↔ root co-occurrence networks (hubs, modules, asymmetry).
15. **Conservation & Motifs** — sequence conservation / recurring motifs ↔ recurrent Qur'anic formulae; degeneracy & robustness.

### Unit F — Edge & synthesis (16–17)
16. **The Addressee** — human (insān) and jinn; the seen (shahāda) and unseen (ghayb); where empirical science goes silent.
17. **Synthesis & Capstone** — the full ladder ending in the human; a complete-pipeline capstone on an unseen root. *(Final after this.)*

**Why this order is natural:** foundations teach *why* and *how* (1–2); we then meet the *simplest substance* — the symbols — and test the first naïve claim about them (3–4); we assemble symbols into sequences with no meaning yet (5–6); meaning enters and then varies (7–9); the units become dynamic — copied, checked, regulated (10–12); we zoom out to whole-system architecture (13–15); and we close at the addressee and a synthesis capstone (16–17). Nothing later is needed to understand anything earlier.
