## The headline result (see `disjoint_letters/REPORT.md`)
After the signal course found only generic structure, the **disjoint letters proved to be a positional/temporal indexing system**: a tag marks a family of sūras **contiguous in muṣḥaf and revelation order** (label-permutation **p = 2×10⁻⁵**, all 29 sūras) and flags the **long** sūras (p = 2×10⁻⁵). They carry **no** shared content or theme (p = 0.27); the frequency claim was a false positive caught by the baseline. **Meta-thesis:** the Qur'an's detectable latent structure is **relational/organizational, not in local content statistics.**
