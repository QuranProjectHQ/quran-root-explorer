## ✶ CLUSTER — القرآن يفسر بعضه بعضا (self-interpretation), diverse subjects to expand
The hermeneutic name for the project's strongest findings (recurrence #42, canonical coherence #57).
Each is a distinct *mode* of the Qur'an interpreting itself; all are data-anchored and ready to expand.

- **6a · The Qur'an Answers Its Own Questions** — the *wa-mā adrāka / mā hiya* self-gloss formula
  (101 al-Qāriʿa; 104:5–7 al-Ḥuṭama; 97:2 laylat al-qadr; 90:12 al-ʿaqaba). Mode: term → deferral →
  definition, intra-text. Anchor: a countable self-reference device; discourse-move sequencing (Lens 10).
- **6b · One Story, Many Tellings** — complementary recurrence: gaps in one telling filled by another
  (Mūsā infancy only in al-Qaṣaṣ; Khiḍr only in al-Kahf; the magicians foregrounded in al-Aʿrāf). Anchor:
  recurrence #42 — short verbatim runs (~2), high reorder (~0.45), 0.5–0.9 band = re-expression, not copy.
- **6c · The General and the Detailed (mujmal & mubayyan)** — ṣalāh/zakāh/ḥajj commanded in general,
  detailed across passages; *uḥkimat … thumma fuṣṣilat* (11:1). Mode: a command defined by its elaborations.
- **6d · Staged Legislation** — the wine sequence 2:219 → 4:43 → 5:90 (and similar): a ruling developed
  across verses, each stage read in light of the next. Mode: self-interpretation across revelation-time.
- **6e · A Term Defined by Its Echoes** — *those favored* (1:7 ↔ 4:69); *the firm handhold* (al-ʿurwa
  al-wuthqā, 2:256 ↔ 31:22); *al-mathānī* (15:87 ↔ 39:23). Mode: a word glossed by its other occurrences.
- **6f · Restriction & Exception across verses (takhṣīṣ / istithnāʾ)** — the general then the carve-out
  (al-ʿAṣr 103: *illā alladhīna āmanū*; forbidden foods 6:145 ↔ the necessity-exception 2:173). Mode: one
  verse bounding another.
- **6g · Question Posed Here, Answered There** — e.g. *what is the soul?* (17:85) vs the breathing-in of
  the rūḥ (15:29; 32:9); *who are the favored / the cursed?* resolved across sūras. Mode: distributed Q&A.

## ☐ Proposed (ready to expand, with anchors)
6. **The Inverse of the Poets** — cross-language finding: masters (Mutanabbī, Shakespeare, Hafez)
   *minimize* long-range repetition; the Qur'an *maximizes* it (#30). The craft axis is the deliberate
   opposite of the poetic master's. Same-language control: Mutanabbī shows the universal master signature.
7. **Half the Alphabet** — the muqaṭṭaʿāt, full arc (#50–56): 14/28 exact; canonical clustering
   (Moran +0.54, robust to nuzūl); root-space cohesion z=+6.9 (register-controlled +7.4); anchored in the
   "the Book" theme (z=+3.55, openings ~5×). Limits: no phonetic-balance cipher (p=0.14), no letter→theme
   cipher (p=0.37). A model of "signal AND its boundaries."
8. **When the Tool Is Too Weak** — the telescope rule: non-detection indicts the instrument, not reality.
   Case study: the recited/phonological layer (vocalized prosody) — instrument built, Qur'an-internal
   rhythm real (isochrony; weight-alternation z=−10.7 in al-Baqara), distinctiveness data-blocked.
   Discipline: "absence of evidence ≠ evidence of absence" (and its twin — never licenses a claim).
9. **What the Sieve Keeps** — how the gate/null works, and what survived vs what died: survived = recurrence,
   rhyme-persistence, muqaṭṭaʿāt; died = whole-surah rings (z≈0), phonosemantics (null), syntactic
   complexity (Qur'an simpler than prose). Rigor as a form of reverence.
10. **The Order of the Book** — canonical-order thematic coherence (#57): adjacent sūras root-similar beyond
    length (z=+3.1, p<10⁻³); the *Ḥawāmīm*, *Ṭawāsīn*, *Musabbiḥāt* as designed neighborhoods; NMF themes.
11. **The Honest Negative** — why we publicly reject attractive claims that fail the gate (phonetic balance
    p=0.14; letter cipher p=0.37; whole-surah rings null). The ethic: conviction guides the search, evidence
    alone decides the claim (DESIGN_STANCE.md).
12. **Divinely-Rooted vs Human Artifact** — why we study the rasm/roots/order and deprioritize the ḥarakāt
    (human notation). Method as theology: measure the revealed object, not the editors.

    A fresh, data-driven face of
    القرآن يفسر بعضه بعضا: the verse's own ENDING interprets the verse. Measured — the āyah-final attribute
    (رحیم/حکیم/قدیر…) statistically predicts the body's content (morphology grain mean z=+12.1, 16/16 classes;

    ordinary Arabic, poetry, or even sajʿ (≥3× share 0.28 vs 0.04; #63). So each verse carries a built-in
    interpretive *seal* at its close, learned from the corpus's own data — self-interpretation made
    quantitative, fusing rhyme × meaning × wazn at the verse-end. Reconcile with #60 (the seal caps its OWN
    verse, doesn't chain to the next). The deeper lesson for the series: we are LEARNING the Qur'an's
    interpretive keys from the Qur'an's data — the empirical form of "the Qur'an explains itself."
